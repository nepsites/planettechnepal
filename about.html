Connecting mountains to the world

We are a dedicated team of surveillance engineers building intelligent CCTV and monitoring systems for the planet’s most remote and demanding places — so anyone, anywhere, can see the mountains in real time.

Our story

Planet Tech Nepal started with a simple frustration: the most breathtaking and important places in the Himalaya were also the hardest to watch over. Power was scarce, networks were thin, and off-the-shelf cameras froze and failed.

So we built our own. Combining deep expertise in IoT, cloud streaming and high-altitude infrastructure, we now deploy intelligent CCTV and weather systems across remote mountain regions and urban landscapes alike.

Our mission

To deliver real-time visibility through secure, ultra-low-latency streaming solutions designed for reliability, scalability and long-term performance — even in extreme environments.

  • Reliability first, in the harshest conditions
  • Security and privacy by design
  • Built to scale from one site to a national network
